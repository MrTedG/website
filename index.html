People · Projects · Purpose

Project
Director

I turn ambitious ideas into work people can believe in, contribute to and deliver.

My musical work is matched by more than fifteen years in project and operational management. I create programmes, direct teams, and administer the relationships, schedules and systems that help arts and education organisations thrive. PRINCE2 training gives structure to the creative instinct: clear purpose, practical plans and thoughtful review.

Taunton Choral Society

As Musical Director, I grew audience reach and engagement by 40%, developed the chorus’s symphonic identity with Southern Sinfonia and initiated performances at Carnegie Hall. Stronger planning, procedures and financial reserves gave the society room to grow.

Wells Cathedral School

In a £1 million specialist music department, I built scheduling and reporting systems for a programme of around 1,000 events a week and served as Product Owner for a new management platform. Better systems supported savings of more than £100,000.

Military Wives Choir Foundation

I worked across regional and national teams, coordinating performance projects and helping deliver the National Poppy Appeal launch with musicians, choirs and venue partners.

Artistic ambition, organised with care.

Artistic direction · Choral music

Conductor

A clear musical vision grows through listening, trust and shared work.

I am Musical Director of Taunton Choral Society, shaping programmes and preparing singers for ambitious choral and orchestral repertoire. My conducting brings together a singer’s attention to language and line with a director’s sense of how an ensemble can move as one.

My previous leadership includes Somerset Choral Society, Berkshire Youth Choir and the Military Wives Choir Foundation. I have also led workshops and projects in the UK and internationally, working with singers at different stages of their musical lives.

Opera · Oratorio · Song

Lyric
Tenor

A voice for the story: attentive to text, colour and character.

A former BBC Singer, I have performed in opera, oratorio, concert and broadcast. Highlights include appearing with Bryn Terfel at the Last Night of the Proms and recordings for Naxos, Signum and Deutsche Grammophon.

My repertoire ranges from Bach’s Passions and major oratorios to opera, art song and contemporary music. I have contributed to BBC broadcasts on Goethe, Peter Pears and Steve Reich’s The Desert Music.

Lessons · Workshops · Masterclasses

Teaching

Help each singer find a confident, expressive voice of their own.

I teach singing at Wells Cathedral School and work with students, ensembles and teachers through one-to-one lessons, workshops and masterclasses. My approach joins healthy technique with a lively curiosity about words, style and musical intention.

My educational work has included the Royal Irish Academy of Music, Marlborough College, Malaysia and the BBC Ten Pieces Project. I also work as an adjudicator and lead sessions in choral singing, vocal confidence, interpretation and performance.
