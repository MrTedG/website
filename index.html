A connected career

Music is the common thread.

Edward Goater’s work moves between the concert platform, classroom and arts organisation.

He is a professional tenor, conductor and teacher with an international performing and educational career. Alongside the music, he has led ensembles, built programmes and managed the people and systems that help creative work thrive.

Musical understanding, care for people and a practical eye for delivery belong in the same room. Edward brings all three to the work.

On stage · In rehearsal · In the room

The music, from every angle.

From shaping a phrase to shaping a programme, the work starts with listening—and grows through people working well together.

01 / Conduct

Conduct

Artistic direction, choral and orchestral conducting, and chorus mastering. Edward is Musical Director of Taunton Choral Society and has worked with ensembles including the BBC Scottish Symphony Orchestra and BBC Philharmonic Orchestra.

Leadership in action ↗
02 / Perform

Sing

A wide-ranging tenor repertoire spanning opera, oratorio, concert and broadcast. A former member of the BBC Singers, Edward has appeared with Bryn Terfel at the Last Night of the Proms and recorded for leading labels.

Listen to selected work ↗
03 / Teach

Teach

One-to-one teaching, workshops, masterclasses and adjudication. Edward teaches at Wells Cathedral School; his educational work has included the Royal Irish Academy of Music, Marlborough College, Malaysia and the BBC Ten Pieces Project.

Project management · Artistic leadership

Good music needs
good leadership.

I create programmes, direct teams and administer the people, partnerships and systems that bring ambitious work to life.

With more than fifteen years in music, education and arts operations, Edward combines an artist’s understanding of the work with a manager’s view of the whole. PRINCE2 training adds a structured approach to planning, delivery and review.

Artistic direction

Taunton Choral Society

Expanded the society’s reach and artistic ambition, developed its symphonic chorus identity with Southern Sinfonia, and initiated performances at Carnegie Hall. The organisation strengthened its goals, procedures and reserves as audience engagement grew.

Music operations

Wells Cathedral School

As Operations Administrator in a £1 million specialist music department, created scheduling and reporting systems for a complex weekly programme and served as Product Owner for a new management platform.

Project delivery

Military Wives Choir Foundation

Supported regional foundations and central teams, coordinated performance projects, and helped deliver the National Poppy Appeal launch with musicians, groups and venue partners.

A musician’s ear for detail. A leader’s responsibility for the whole.
